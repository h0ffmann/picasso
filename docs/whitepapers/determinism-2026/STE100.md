# ASD-STE100 passes on WHITEPAPER.md

`WHITEPAPER.md` at the repo root is the paper of `whitepaper.html` (printed as `PICASSOPAPER.pdf`)
in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, issue 9. Two passes made it, each with the rules of
[`/ste100`](../../../.claude/commands/ste100.md), on 2026-10-12. `just render ste` prints it to
`WHITEPAPER.pdf`.

These items did not change in the two passes: the References section (byte for byte), each code
span, each citation marker (the same markers, the same number of times, on the same claims), each
number with its unit, each quotation, the headings and the author, ORCID and "Cite as" lines.

## Pass 1: rewrite

Two agents rewrote the paper in two halves (title to §4, §5 to §9).

- They split compound sentences into short sentences with one topic each. A citation marker stays
  at the end of the group of sentences that it supports.
- They split each paragraph of more than six sentences (§3.1 to §3.4, §4, §9).
- They removed -ing forms ("stops working" became "does not work any more", "self-improving
  factory" became "a factory that improves itself", "Pinning versus patching" became "Pins versus
  patches").
- They used only simple tenses ("has become" became "is now", "would have shipped" was removed).
- They removed phrasal verbs ("tell apart" became "distinguish", "written up" was removed).
- They used the active voice where the agent is known ("References were found" became "We found
  the references"; Experiments A and B now say "We fetched" and "We built").
- They used approved, simpler words ("routinely" became "frequently", "converge" became "occur at
  the same time", "tension" became "conflict").
- They put the steps of P2, P3 and §7 in the imperative, with the condition first.

## Pass 2: check

One agent read the full text against the rules and against the original. It made 17 edits.

- One term for one meaning in the two halves: "of picasso" for ownership (3), "spec" for the
  Yggdrasil document (1), "different completions" (1).
- Word forms and phrasal verbs: "a moving pointer", "would ship", "go into", "stay fixed" (4).
- One repeated "Thus" (1), and the Background paragraph of the abstract cut to six sentences (1).
- Five meanings that pass 1 had made weaker or lost, restored from the original: "never builds
  itself in place", "no longer", "its own tools", "can remain a Dockerfile", "should be stated"
  (not "must").

## Kept outside the rules

- Three sentences are longer than 25 words because each holds a verbatim quotation or a citation
  marker: the north-star measure in §1, the journal quotation in §5.2 item 3, and the coding-agent
  sentence in the abstract.
- The reproducible-builds.org definition in Table 2 is a verbatim quotation.
- Some -ing words stay: inside quotations ("running", "including", "drawing up"), in names
  ("Thinking Machines Lab", `writing-styles`), in technical terms ("coding agents", "routing") and
  as approved nouns ("warning", "meaning").
